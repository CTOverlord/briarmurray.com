How Replay works

Nothing is uploaded. Clips are read on this device only, and the originals are never changed.

Open
Pick a clip from your phone. Pick two at once to see them side by side.
Step
‹ 1 and 1 › move one frame, ‹ 5 and 5 › move five. Hold a button down to keep going, so holding ‹ 1 runs the clip slowly backwards.
Strip
Drag the film strip under the picture to inch through frames.
Speed
Normal, 1/2, 1/4 or 1/8.
Moment
Start and End mark a moment and show the time between them. Loop replays it.
Zoom
Pinch the picture, drag to look around, then Reset zoom. Flip mirrors the picture, handy for a left turn against a right turn, or a left-hander against a right-hander.
Compare
Open a second clip, step each one to the same moment (ball release, a gate), then Link clips so they play and step together. Tap a clip to choose which one the buttons move.
Keys
Space plays and pauses, ← and → step one frame, Shift with an arrow steps five, [ and ] set Start and End, L loops.

Film at 60 frames a second for twice as many frames to step through. The bouncing ball is a sample to try the buttons on.
